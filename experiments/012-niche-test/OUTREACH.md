# Outreach Playbook — Experiment 012

## 0. Set up before the first pitch (one evening)
- **Separate business identity.** Use a business Instagram account and a business email (e.g. a Gmail or custom domain). Don't pitch from a personal or persona account.
- **Simple portfolio.** The business Instagram itself, or a one-page site (Carrd/Notion), with 6–9 of your best demo pieces. All demo pieces must use products you own or have permission to use, or be clearly fictional.
- **One-line offer:** "AI product photos and short videos for small brands — 10 images + 2 clips in 5 days."

## 1. Where to find prospects

### A. Small product brands
- **Instagram:** search niche hashtags plus your region (e.g. `#shopsmallcanada`, `#handmadeontario`, `#[city]smallbusiness`, plus the product type). Target accounts with roughly 500–20k followers, active posting, and weak or repetitive product photos.
- **Etsy:** search the product type, then filter shop location to Canada or Ontario.
- **Shopify stores:** Google `site:myshopify.com candles Ontario` (change the product and region).
- **Local markets:** vendor lists from farmers' and makers' markets and holiday pop-ups.

### B. Real estate agents
- **Realtor.ca:** look for listings with **empty or poorly furnished rooms**; those are staging candidates. The listing agent's name and brokerage are on the page.
- **Brokerage websites** list agent emails and Instagram handles.
- **Instagram:** search `#[city]realestate` or `#[city]realtor`. Newer agents who post often are the best targets.
- Real estate boards and brokerages have rules on altered listing photos. Virtual staging must be disclosed as such. Check the agent's board rules before delivering, and put "virtually staged" on every staged image.

### C. Clothing boutiques
- Instagram and Google Maps for independent boutiques in your area.
- **Walk-ins work well here.** Show samples on your phone.

## 2. Channels
| Channel | Best for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram DM | Product brands, boutiques | Personalised, with 2 sample images attached. Many owners check DMs more than email |
| Email (address published on their site) | Real estate, Shopify brands | Subject line: "Made a few photos of your [product]" |
| Walk-in | Local boutiques and shops | Avoid busy hours; leave a card or QR code to your portfolio |

**Canadian anti-spam law (CASL):**
- **Who to email:** generally only addresses that are publicly posted, and only about their business.
- **What to include:** your name, contact info and an easy way to opt out (e.g. "Reply 'no thanks' and I won't contact you again").
- **Volume:** keep it one-to-one, never bulk blasts.
- This is not legal advice. Read the CRTC's CASL guidance before scaling up.

## 3. Message templates (personalise the first line every time)

### Product brand (DM)
> Hi [name]! Love your [specific product], [one genuine detail]. I make AI product photos and short videos for small brands, so I made a couple of yours to show what's possible (attached). If you'd like a set of 10 + 2 short clips for your feed or ads, I can turn it around in 5 days for [price]. No pressure at all!

### Real estate (email)
> Subject: Virtually staged your listing at [street]
>
> Hi [name], I noticed [address] has a few empty rooms, so I virtually staged one to show the difference (attached, clearly marked as virtually staged). I offer staging plus short social walkthrough clips made from your listing photos, with 48-hour turnaround from [price per listing]. Happy to do one more room free if it's useful.
>
> [Your name] · [phone] · [portfolio link]
> Not interested? Just reply "no thanks" and I won't follow up.

### Boutique (DM or walk-in)
> Hi! I really like the [garment] you posted. I make AI styled product shots for boutiques, so I put together two versions of it (attached). If you'd like a set for your next drop, I can do 10 images + 2 clips for [price].

### Follow-up (once, 4–5 days later)
> Hi [name], just checking you saw these. Happy to make one more using any product you choose, free. If now's not the time, no worries!

## 4. Honesty rules for every job
- Tell clients plainly that the content is AI-generated.
- Never change the product's real appearance, features or condition.
- Real estate: staged images are labelled "virtually staged"; walkthrough clips must not invent rooms, views or features.
- Client gets commercial use of delivered images. You keep the right to show them in your portfolio *only with permission*.
- Check that each tool you use allows commercial use on your plan.
